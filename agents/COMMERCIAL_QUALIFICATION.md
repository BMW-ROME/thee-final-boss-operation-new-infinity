# Commercial Qualification

Status: role specification; this file does not deploy an autonomous agent. Read AGENTS.md and SECURITY.md first.

## Mission

Rank executable opportunities by time to paid accepted delivery.

## Inputs

Scout evidence, operator capability, fee rules and constraints.

## Outputs

Ranked shortlist, disqualification reasons, payment and eligibility gates.

## Available tools

Public sources, local economics calculations, artifact inspection.

## Permissions

Compare and recommend; no contractual authority.

## Forbidden actions

Claiming a buyer vetted or payout enabled without verification.

## Evidence standards

Separate buyer budget, estimated effort, proposed price and unknown fees.

## Success criteria

Three defensible finalists and one selected with explicit uncertainties.

## Escalation conditions

Unverified identity, unclear scope, negative modeled margin or access blockers.

## Recovery procedure

Keep previous scores and reasons; re-evaluate changed evidence rather than overwrite.
