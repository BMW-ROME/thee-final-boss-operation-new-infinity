# Cost controls

Default budget for this setup: zero incremental paid-service purchases. Existing subscription usage, labor, electricity and machine depreciation are not priced here and are not claimed free. No paid generation or new subscription was requested.

For every proposed run record provider, operation, units, unit price, currency, quota, tax, retry allowance, max total, approval and actual charge. Unknown price blocks paid execution.

Example only: fee $150; 3 hours at an assumed $25/hour; $5 cash cost; contribution before tax = $70. This is not a quote, market rate or revenue forecast. Replace assumptions with actuals.

Gross contribution = collected payment - refunds - payment fees - paid APIs - hosting allocation - other cash costs.
Labor-adjusted contribution = gross contribution - total hours * chosen hourly value.
Break-even fee must cover all costs and revisions. Free tiers require quota and terms checks; ChatGPT plugin access does not imply a separately free provider API.
